🔒 Secure Smart Escrow Escrow Contract

  1. When you search and click Connect, the cost share is transferred from your wallet to a secure Escrow hold.
  2. This ensures the traveler knows the ride is funded before starting the trip.
  3. Once the delivery/ride is completed, the sender can Release the funds to the traveler.
  4. If the connection is cancelled, the funds are Refunded back to the sender's wallet.
  5. Cost-sharing is restricted to expense-sharing only to ensure full regulatory compliance.
